# 🎬 Film Rental & Revenue Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive, end-to-end Business Intelligence dashboard built with **Power BI** to analyze movie rental operations, revenue streams, customer distribution, and film inventory trends.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project provides actionable business insights for a movie rental service. It tracks key performance indicators (KPIs) such as total revenue, rental counts, film duration, customer retention, and regional market trends.

### 🔑 Key Metrics Analyzed:
* **Total Revenue:** ~$67.41K
* **Total Rentals:** 129M+
* **Total Film Titles:** 1,000
* **Total Actors Tracked:** 200
* **Average Rental Rate:** 4.99

---

## 🚀 Key Dashboard Features

1. **Executive Summary & Overview:** 
   - Top-level KPI cards for quick business health monitoring.
   - Monthly revenue trends and overall operational efficiency.

2. **Genre & Category Trends:**
   - Visual breakdown of rental demand across genres (Sports, Sci-Fi, Drama, Comedy, etc.).
   - Film rating distribution (PG-13, NC-17, PG, R, G).

3. **Geographic & Customer Analysis:**
   - Customer concentration and market share by country and city.
   - Top revenue-generating regions (e.g., India, China, US).

4. **Film & Actor Insights:**
   - Inventory utilization by title and genre.
   - Actor popularity metrics and revenue contribution per actor.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* **Business Intelligence Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Visualizations:** Treemaps, Donut Charts, Stacked Bar Charts, Line/Area Charts, KPI Cards
* **Data Sources:** Film Rental Relational Database (SQL/Excel export)
